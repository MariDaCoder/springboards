## Status definitions

- **Requirement identified:** The requirement is documented, but evidence is not confirmed ready or submitted.
- **Evidence ready/submitted:** The evidence is assembled or has been sent; written approval is still pending.
- **Approval or activation confirmed:** The agency, partner, or program owner has confirmed approval, activation, or acceptance in writing.

## Program launch statuses

Use this separate status for deciding what can be promoted publicly:

- **Ready to promote:** Eligibility, funding, location, dates, contact path, and required forms are confirmed.
- **In preparation:** Documents or approvals are still pending.
- **Future program:** Concept only; do not advertise as available.
