# Apprenticeship Program Readiness

**Evidence status:** Requirement identified; sponsor and registration pathway are not confirmed.  
**Program launch status:** **Future program**  
**Source set:** OJT/Apprenticeship email thread, program materials, workforce roadmap, tracker.

## Who qualifies

Eligibility depends on the selected apprenticeship sponsor, occupation standards, employer requirements, and participant intake process. Confirm age, education, employment, and other requirements with the sponsor before publishing them.

## What funding supports it

Potential support may include employer wages, workforce funding, tuition assistance, or sponsor-supported training. No funding source should be advertised until a sponsor agreement and funding terms are confirmed.

## Where it happens

At approved Springboards classroom/lab facilities and employer worksites listed in the apprenticeship standards and sponsor agreement.

## When it starts

Not confirmed. Start dates depend on sponsor approval, registered standards, employer placements, instructor readiness, and participant selection.

## What participants need

- Sponsor application and eligibility documents
- Any required education, identification, or employment documents
- Safety orientation and required PPE
- Attendance and work-hour commitments
- Progress documentation required by the sponsor

## Who refers them

The approved apprenticeship sponsor, employer partners, Career Centers, and workforce agencies as applicable. The sponsor and referral process are still to be identified.
