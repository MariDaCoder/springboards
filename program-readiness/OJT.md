## Who qualifies

Eligibility depends on the administering workforce program and the participant’s Career Center determination. Do not promise eligibility from the website. Confirm whether participants must be referred, enrolled, or approved through a Career Center.

## What funding supports it

OJT materials describe employer-supported training and wage reimbursement for eligible hires. The tracker references STAARS setup and potential reimbursement. Confirm the reimbursement percentage, eligible wages, contract term, and participant limits in the executed agreement.

## Where it happens

At approved Springboards worksites and participating employer/project locations listed in the OJT contract.

## When it starts

Not confirmed. Start dates require an approved contract, eligible participant referral, insurance, payroll/timekeeping readiness, and worksite approval.

## What participants need

- Career Center eligibility/referral, if required
- Required identity and hiring documents
- Employer onboarding documents
- Safety orientation and required PPE
- Any program-specific assessment or credential requirements

## Who refers them

The administering workforce agency and local Career Center determine referral and eligibility. Confirm the named agency contact and current referral process.
