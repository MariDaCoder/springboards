# STAARS Vendor Registration Readiness

**Status:** Requirement identified; vendor activation is not confirmed.  
**Source set:** STAARS presentation, tracker, Alicia Ford correspondence, OJT materials.

## Who qualifies

STAARS is an organizational vendor/payment registration process rather than a participant program. Springboards must meet the state’s vendor and documentation requirements.

## What funding supports it

STAARS registration supports state payment and reimbursement administration. It does not by itself approve OJT, Transitional Jobs, ETPL, or apprenticeship participation.

## Where it happens

Administrative registration occurs through the State of Alabama vendor/accounting process. Program work occurs at separately approved Springboards worksites.

## When it starts

After the required banking, tax, insurance, and vendor documents are accepted and the vendor record/code is activated.

## What participants need

Not applicable. The organization needs:

- Business checking account under the exact legal name
- Signed bank verification letter
- Current W-9
- Workers’ compensation certificate, if required for the related program/vendor setup
- Any additional state vendor forms requested by Alicia Ford

## Who refers them

Not applicable. Alicia Ford is identified in the tracker as the STAARS contact; confirm the current email and submission instructions.
