# Individual Training Accounts (ITA) / Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL) Readiness

**Evidence status:** Evidence ready/submitted in parts; provider approval is not confirmed.  
**Program launch status:** **In preparation**  
**Source set:** ETPL Policy 2025 Change 2, New WIOA Training Provider Guide, ETPL email threads, program materials, tracker.

## Who qualifies

Participants must meet WIOA/ITA eligibility requirements and receive approval through the responsible Career Center or workforce agency. Springboards should not promise ITA funding before provider and participant approval.

## What funding supports it

An approved ETPL provider may be eligible for tuition support through Individual Training Accounts. The approved program, credential, cost, duration, and participant eligibility determine what funding is available.

## Where it happens

At the approved Springboards training facility and any approved classroom, lab, or worksite locations listed in the provider application.

## When it starts

Not confirmed. Training start dates should follow provider approval, participant ITA authorization, enrollment, and documented scheduling.

## What participants need

- Career Center intake and eligibility determination
- ITA authorization before enrollment, if required
- Identity and eligibility documents requested by the Career Center
- Enrollment paperwork
- Attendance and progress commitments

## Who refers them

Local Career Center staff and workforce agency representatives. The tracker identifies Jill Grant as the ETPL contact; confirm the current application contact details before submission.
