## Who qualifies

Participants must be screened through an eligible local Career Center. The current notes identify Valley, Opelika, and Phenix City Career Centers. The participant process includes identity documentation, eligibility review, and an employment assessment. Confirm the current eligibility checklist with Career Center staff before publishing it.

## What funding supports it

The Transitional Jobs/OJT materials describe subsidized paid training and a pathway toward employment. Current tracker notes identify a 390-hour lifetime limit and up to 32 hours per week. Confirm the current funding terms, wage, reimbursement, and participant limits with the administering agency.

## Where it happens

At an approved Springboards worksite and participating project locations. The Worksite Agreement and agency approval determine which locations and duties are authorized.

## When it starts

Not confirmed. Participant start dates should be scheduled only after Career Center eligibility verification, referral, onboarding, and worksite approval.

## What participants need

- State photo ID
- Social Security card
- Proof of SNAP or unemployment assistance, if applicable
- Career Center employment assessment
- Required onboarding and payroll documents
- Any worksite-required safety equipment

## Who refers them

Local Career Center staff refer eligible participants to Springboards after intake and verification. Confirm the active referral contacts for Valley, Opelika, and Phenix City.
