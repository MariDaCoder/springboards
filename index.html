OUR MISSION

BuildingPathways.CreatingPossibilities.

Springboards for Community Development empowers returning citizens, justice-involved individuals, and opportunity youth across the West Georgia and East Alabama corridor by providing rigorous, debt-free vocational training in high-demand construction trades. Through hands-on apprenticeship lab mastery, verifiable industry credentials, 1:1 peer mentorship, and comprehensive wrap-around support, Springboards expands viable economic options, breaks the cycle of recidivism, and builds sustainable, living-wage careers.

Train

On-the-job training and transitional jobs that connect learning to work.

Train

Transitional Jobs and OJT provide a supported bridge into employment. Available materials describe state-subsidized training wages, up to 32 hours weekly, a 390-hour lifetime cap, and a pathway toward permanent placement.

  • Start with eligibility verification through a career center.
  • Career center staff refer eligible participants to a participating worksite.
  • Bring questions about current openings, schedule, and worksite placement.
