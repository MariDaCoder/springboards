THE REALITY IN EAST ALABAMA

Too many people are stuck before they ever get a fair chance.

Many people in East Alabama face limited access to training, weak job pathways, and barriers that keep them from building stable work. For too many families, the challenge is not lack of effort — it is lack of access.

“Talent is everywhere in Chambers County and East Alabama—what has been missing is a tuition-free workshop where people can learn a real trade without going into debt.”

— Springboards Workforce Initiative • Shawmut, AL

Limited access to career training

Local workers often cannot afford costly tuition or travel far outside Chambers County to learn a dependable trade.

Few clear pathways into skilled work

Without industry certifications or jobsite connections, motivated people get locked out of living-wage construction careers.

03

Barriers that make stable employment harder to reach

WHAT PEOPLE LEARN

Skills that lead to real opportunity.

Practical, high-demand construction and mechanical skills taught through hands-on shop and jobsite practice.

01

Carpentry and construction basics

Framing, measurement, blueprint reading, drywall installation, and safe hand and power tool operation.

02

Electrical fundamentals

Residential wiring basics, circuit testing, breaker panels, conduit installation, and electrical safety.

03

Plumbing and pipefitting

Copper and PVC fitting, fixture installation, pipe layout, drainage fundamentals, and system maintenance.

04

HVAC and mechanical systems

Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning fundamentals, system airflow, and EPA 608 certification prep.

05

Jobsite safety and certifications

OSHA 10/30 construction safety protocols, PPE readiness, hazard recognition, and NCCER core credentials.

06

Teamwork, discipline, and work readiness

Jobsite communication, timekeeping, reliability, problem-solving, and interview preparation for long-term employment.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions about free trade training at Springboards.

Is the training really free?

Yes. Every Springboards training pathway is 100% tuition-free for participants. Instruction, tools, personal protective equipment (PPE), and certification testing are covered through workforce grants, community partners, and donors.

Who is eligible?

We serve young adults (ages 18–24), jobseekers, returning citizens, and underemployed adults across Shawmut, Lanett, Valley, and East Alabama who are ready to show up, learn a skilled trade, and move toward stable employment.

Do I need experience?

No prior trade or construction experience is required. We start with the fundamentals—tool safety, measuring, and core trade skills—and guide you step by step.

What skills are taught?

You will learn hands-on carpentry and construction basics, electrical fundamentals, plumbing and pipefitting, HVAC and mechanical systems, OSHA jobsite safety and NCCER certifications, and professional work readiness.

Where are programs held?

Training is held at the Springboards Community Center, located at 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863 (Shawmut). Training sessions and project blocks run Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

How do I apply?

Click Apply for Training on this page and complete the brief candidate form. An advisor will contact you within 1–2 business days to discuss your goals and help you complete any needed Alabama Career Center steps.

What happens after training?

Graduates receive guided support into WIOA Transitional Jobs, On-the-Job Training (OJT), apprenticeship pathways, and referrals to local construction and trade employers across East Alabama.

Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: September 2026

Springboards for Community Development is dedicated to protecting the privacy of prospective students, participants, volunteers, and donors.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily submit through our program inquiry, volunteer signup, donation, or direct contact triggers, including your name, telephone number, email address, availability, and trade preferences.

2. How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used exclusively to contact you regarding vocational training enrollment, volunteer project scheduling, partner inquiries, or deductible donation receipts. We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to external advertisers or telemarketers.

3. Equal Opportunity & Equality

Springboards provides training and career services regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or background, in accordance with applicable federal and state guidelines.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You may request to view, modify, or delete your contact records from our database at any time by emailing info@springboardsfcd.org or calling 615-603-1157.
