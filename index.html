THE REALITY WE FACE

Breaking the Cycle Takes More Than Good Intentions.

For too many people across Shawmut, Lanett, Valley, and throughout East Alabama, the path to stable work is blocked before it ever begins.

  • Strict barriers to employment for returning citizens and low income neighborhoods.
  • A severe shortage of accessible, informative vocational trade training across Alabama.
  • Employment with no growth potential that make true financial independence out of reach.
“Bad choices produce bad results. Making better choices is virtually impossible when realistically available options never change.” Marvin Thompson
OUR MISSION

BuildingPathways.CreatingPossibilities.

Springboards for Community Development is proudly based in Shawmut, Alabama, dedicated to serving returning citizens, justice-involved individuals, at-risk youth, and underemployed residents across East Alabama. Through WIOA-authorized programs, we provide rigorous, debt-free vocational training in high-demand construction trades, opening viable economic pathways, breaking cycles of recidivism, and building sustainable, living-wage careers through:

  • Hands-on apprenticeship lab mastery
  • Verifiable industry credentials
  • 1:1 peer mentorship and comprehensive wrap-around support
  • Clear pathways to sustainable, living-wage careers

ABOUT THE GUIDE

Marvin Thompson

Executive Director & Founder, Springboards for Community Development

As Founder and Executive Director of Springboards for Community Development, Marvin Thompson established the academy out of a deep personal commitment to community uplifting, dedicating the program in loving memory to his late daughter, Shawtisha LaChelle Thompson. Guided by the conviction that breaking cycles of poverty and recidivism requires expanding realistically available economic options, Mr. Thompson leads daily operations and workforce partnerships at the Shawmut training facility. Under his direction, Springboards spearheads WIOA Transitional Jobs and On-the-Job Training with the Alabama Career Center and Sirius Workforce LLC, equipping returning citizens, at-risk youth, and underemployed community members with verified mastery over high-demand construction trades, nationally recognized credentials, and sustainable living-wage careers.

“You can either praise us or criticize us, we’re still gonna be about our business of community uplifting.” Marvin Thompson, Executive Director

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our debt-free trade programs, worksite partnerships, and community training.

How much does training cost at Springboards?

Every Springboards vocational program is 100% debt-free to participants. Tuition, certification testing fees, personal protective equipment, work boots, and practice materials are fully sponsored through grant funding, corporate sponsorships, and generous community donors.

Who is eligible to participate in Springboards programs?

Springboards is based in Shawmut, Alabama, and primarily serves Alabama residents across Chambers County and East Alabama. Through WIOA-authorized programs in partnership with the Alabama Career Center, we specifically welcome returning citizens, justice-involved individuals, at-risk youth ages 18 to 24, and underemployed adults seeking viable, debt-free career pathways in the skilled construction trades.

What credentials and certifications can I earn?

Depending on your chosen trade pathway, you work toward industry credentials that employers actively require, including:

  • NCCER Core & Craft Credentials: Carpentry, Electrical, Masonry, Plumbing, and HVAC
  • OSHA 10-Hour & 30-Hour Construction Safety credentials
  • EPA 608 Universal Certification for HVAC refrigerant handling
  • First Aid / CPR / AED workplace safety certifications
Where and when are programs held?

Programs operate directly out of our center in Shawmut at the Springboards Community Center, 2269 15th Ave. S.W., Lanett, AL 36863. Serving Shawmut, Lanett, Valley, and surrounding Alabama communities, WIOA training blocks, trade workshops, and volunteer shifts run Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

How does the Transitional Jobs & OJT model work?

Under the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act, participants are verified through the Alabama Career Center in Valley, AL, and referred to participating host worksites. Wages are state-subsidized through Sirius Workforce LLC, providing real-world job site experience under seasoned trade mentors with a direct bridge to 50% wage-reimbursed On-the-Job Training and permanent employment.

How can local employers and volunteers get involved?

Employers can partner with Springboards as host worksites or hiring partners by emailing info@springboardsfcd.org. Volunteers can register for community service blocks to help renovate center facilities, prep trade tools, and assist with neighborhood revitalization projects.

Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: September 2026

Springboards for Community Development is dedicated to protecting the privacy of prospective students, participants, volunteers, and donors.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily submit through our program inquiry, volunteer signup, donation, or direct contact triggers, including your name, telephone number, email address, availability, and trade preferences.

2. How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used exclusively to contact you regarding vocational training enrollment, volunteer project scheduling, partner inquiries, or tax-deductible donation receipts. We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to third-party advertisers or telemarketers.

3. Equal Opportunity & Non-Discrimination

Springboards provides training and career services regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or past justice involvement, in accordance with applicable federal and state guidelines.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You may request to view, modify, or delete your contact records from our database at any time by emailing info@springboardsfcd.org or calling 615-603-1157.
