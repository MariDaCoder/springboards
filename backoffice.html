Approved Entry-Level Job Descriptions

WIOA Non-Supervisory Helper Positions:

The following entry-level positions are structured specifically for the Transitional Jobs and On-the-Job Training pathways in compliance with Career Center directives:

1. Carpenter Helper

Assists lead carpenters with structural framing, measurement, material handling, and assembly while adhering to strict fall protection and power tool safety standards.

2. Electrician Helper

Assists licensed electricians with conduit bending, wire pulling, staging equipment, and lockout/tagout safety compliance under direct supervision.

3. Plumber Helper

Aids plumbing professionals with pipe staging, trench safety precautions, pressure testing observation, and tool calibration.

4. Tool & Inventory Clerk

Tracks worksite equipment, checks tool safety guards and calibrations, and maintains daily equipment logs for project audit readiness.

5. Material Handler / General Laborer

Performs physical worksite preparation, heavy lifting protocol execution, hazard cleanup, and logistics support.

Timesheet & Safety Protocol Guide

Key Participation Guidelines:

  • 390-Hour Lifetime Maximum: Participants are limited to a maximum of 390 subsidized hours under Transitional Jobs policy.
  • 32-Hour Weekly Ceiling: Participants may not exceed 32 hours in any single payroll week.
  • Supervisor Sign-Off: All timesheets must be initialed daily by the designated field supervisor and verified in Avionte / Sirius Staffing.
  • Mandatory Safety PPE: Hard hats, high-visibility vests, eye protection, and work boots are required at all times when on site.
